Formula 1 (F1) is the highest class of international single-seater, open-wheel racing, combining cutting-edge technology, extreme speed, and global competition.
What is Formula 1?
Formula 1 is the pinnacle of motorsport, featuring the fastest regulated road-course racing cars in the world, capable of speeds exceeding 200 mph (322 km/h) and generating extreme G-forces for drivers
